Our services

Assurance and advisory, delivered with Big Four rigour

We focus on work that demands independent judgement. We do not prepare financial statements or sign audit reports. We support audit teams and management with the technical work behind them, keeping our role objective and free of self-review.

01

External Audit Support Services

Offshore support for audit firms and engagement teams, prepared to International Standards on Auditing. The signing auditor keeps responsibility for the opinion.

  • Audit file setup and audit planning
  • Audit risk assessment and internal controls evaluation
  • Design and performance of audit procedures
  • Audit working papers and documentation
02

Internal Audit & Governance

Building the control environment from the ground up — policies, procedures and risk-based internal audit that boards can rely on.

  • Policy and procedure manuals (SOPs)
  • Risk and control matrices
  • Outsourced and co-sourced internal audit
03

Financial Due Diligence

Buy-side and sell-side diligence that gets to the real earnings, cash and risk behind a deal.

  • Quality of earnings and normalised EBITDA
  • Net debt, working capital and debt-like items
  • Vendor due diligence and deal readiness
04

Valuation Services

Defensible valuations for transactions, financial reporting and strategic decisions, built on CFA-grade analysis.

  • Business and equity valuation (DCF, multiples)
  • Purchase price allocation under IFRS 3
  • Impairment testing support (IAS 36)
05

IFRS 9 ECL Modelling

Expected credit loss models that stand up to auditor and regulator scrutiny — designed, built, documented and validated.

  • PD, LGD and EAD methodology
  • Staging criteria and macroeconomic overlays
  • Simplified approach and provision matrices
